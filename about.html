MEET OUR FOUNDER

Zab Acosta

Founder • Certified Mozhzheri Instructor

Founded by Zab Acosta in 2021, Glam U Salon began as a humble 6-square-meter nail studio in Ayala Central Bloc during the pandemic. What started as the dream of a passionate nail artist has grown into a trusted beauty brand with four branches across Cebu. Committed to continuous learning and excellence, Zab completed nail training in Tokyo, Japan, and became a Certified Mozhzheri Instructor through the Mozhzheri School of Nail Aesthetics in Russia. Together with a dedicated team of talented lash and nail artists, guided by experienced trainers and senior technicians, Glam U Salon continues to innovate by combining international techniques with exceptional client care to provide every guest with a premium beauty experience.
