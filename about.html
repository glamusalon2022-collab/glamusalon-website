ABOUT GLAM U SALON

Premium Nails and Lashes.

Since 2021, Glam U Salon has been delivering premium nail and lash services with skilled artistry and exceptional care.

OUR STORY

Beauty with Passion & Excellence

Founded in 2021, Glam U Salon was built with one goal—to provide high-quality beauty services in a clean, relaxing, and friendly environment. Every treatment is performed with precision, premium products, and a commitment to exceptional customer care.

From timeless nail designs and flawless Signature Lash Extensions to advanced Russian Manicure and Russian Pedicure techniques, we continuously invest in education and innovation to ensure every client receives the highest standard of service.

Our Mission

To provide every client with a luxurious beauty experience through exceptional service, continuous education, strict hygiene standards, and outstanding craftsmanship.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted and respected beauty salon brands in the Philippines, recognized for innovation, professionalism, and excellence in nail and lash artistry.
