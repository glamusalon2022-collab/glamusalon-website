MEET OUR FOUNDER

Zab Acosta

Founder • Certified Mozhzheri Instructor

Founded by Zab Acosta in 2021, Glam U Salon began as a humble 6 sqm nail studio in Ayala Central Bloc during the pandemic. What started from the determination of a passionate nail artist has grown into four branches across Cebu. After completing training in Tokyo, Japan, and earning certification as a Mozhzheri Instructor from Russia, Zab continues to lead the salon with innovation, passion, and a commitment to delivering exceptional beauty experiences for every client.
