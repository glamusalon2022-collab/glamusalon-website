MEET OUR FOUNDER

Zab Acosta

Founder • Certified Mozhzheri Instructor

Founded by Zab Acosta in 2021, Glam U Salon began as a 6-square-meter nail studio at Ayala Central Bloc during the pandemic.

Guided by a passion for quality and continuous learning, Zab trained in Tokyo, Japan, and became a Certified Mozhzheri Instructor through the Mozhzheri School of Nail Aesthetics.

Today, together with a dedicated team of skilled nail and lash artists, Glam U Salon continues to provide thoughtful, high-quality beauty experiences across its four branches in Cebu.
